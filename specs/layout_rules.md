# Layout Rules

## Overall Page Style

Each inside page should feel like a single continuous composition rather than separate calendars.

## Inside Page Structure

- 2 columns per page
- 3 months per column
- 6 months total per page

## Column Behavior

- Weekday names appear once at the top of each column
- Dates continue seamlessly down the column across month boundaries
- Non-month dates are omitted rather than repeated
- Month starts are identified by a small inline month label

## Cover Page Structure

- One single date box only
- Date box is centered near the optical center of the page
- Story text appears above it
- Page should remain mostly open for later artistic decoration

## Visual Treatment

- Calendar acts as a background layer for calligraphy and decoration
- Vertical lines are allowed and currently working well
- Horizontal guides should remain subtle
- Avoid heavy boxes or strong compartment boundaries on inside pages
